## Claim strategy

**We do not have social proof and we do not need it yet.** We have *product* proof, and product
proof is stronger at this stage: a real clip, the real prompt, the real stack, a real download.
The TrustMRR data point is the reason — the competitor's launch with a huge audience produced
$58 in all-time revenue. The page's job is not to manufacture trust; it is to be so specific
about what the thing does that trust is not required.

**Never fabricate.** A missing claim is a hole in the page; a fake claim is a lawsuit and a
ruined reputation. If a section would need a number we do not have, cut the section.
