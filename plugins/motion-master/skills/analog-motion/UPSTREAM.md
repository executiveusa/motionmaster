# Upstream

analog-motion is written for Motion Master. It gathers mechanics from three
upstream projects and none of their files are vendored here.

- 12fps stepped clock, and the posterise-the-overlay rule:
  **github.com/iart-ai/map-animation-skills** (MIT, iart.ai)
- The boiling paper (sway and pulse), and the ASR word-to-beat segmentation:
  **github.com/alisa0808/vox-director** (MIT, Alisa0808)
- The reveal techniques and the split-granularity rules:
  **github.com/iart-ai/kinetic-typography-skills** (MIT, iart.ai)

Each of those projects ships its own licence with its own skill; this skill
describes the mechanics and the implementation here is ours.
