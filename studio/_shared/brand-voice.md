# The house voice

How the studio writes, when nobody has said otherwise. A brand record
overrides this with its own `voice.md`.

## The rules

1. **Short sentences.** One idea a sentence.
2. **Concrete over abstract.** "Arrives the night before" beats "enhances your
   morning routine" every time.
3. **Say the thing, then stop.** The ad is not a paragraph; the primary text
   is not an essay.
4. **No borrowed adjectives.** Not "seamless", not "elevated", not
   "game-changing". If the word would fit on any other product's page, it
   belongs to no product.
5. **No triad.** Three things counted in a row — "Fast. Simple. Free." — is a
   pattern, not a sentence. It is the single most common tell of a piece of
   advertising written by nobody.
6. **No false scarcity, no invented numbers, no invented names.** If it is not
   in the brief, it does not go in.
7. **Numbers are written out with their unit.** "32% of users" not "32%".

## Register

Plain, warm, specific. The studio writes the way a good studio partner
would talk to a founder about their product: no theatre, no adjectives
standing in for facts.
