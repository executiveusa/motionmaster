# Where do I go

This is the studio's map. `AGENTS.md` at the top says what the studio is; this
file says which folder to open for a job.

## The pipelines

| the job | the pipeline | what comes out |
|---|---|---|
| a Facebook, Instagram or Reels ad | `01_meta-ad/` | a placement-ready MP4, the copy, the preflight |
| a brand film, a launch film, a commercial | `02_brand-film/` | a 16:9 film, cut to a beat grid |
| cut a recording into shorts | `03_cutdown/` | the cut, with the transcript that drove it |
| a diagram or a concept explainer | `04_explainer/` | an animated schematic |

Each pipeline is a sequence of numbered folders. Each folder has a
`CONTEXT.md` that says what it reads, what it does, what it writes and what a
person checks. Follow the numbering.

## The records

`records/` holds one folder per brand. A record carries the brand's kit, its
voice, its assets and its campaigns. A campaign is a copy of
`_templates/campaign/`, never a blank page.

## The factory

`_shared/` holds the rules that do not change between runs: the house look,
Meta's real numbers, how to write the words, the voice. Read these once per
job; they do not change per campaign.
